Cargo Lamp Bulb

REMOVAL
1. Insert a flat blade screwdriver in slots provided at lower portion of lens.
2. Rotate screwdriver upward until lens snaps out of housing.
3. Remove lens from housing.
4. Remove bulb from bulb socket.

INSTALLATION
1. Position bulb in socket and press into place.
2. Insert upper tabs of lens into lens housing.
3. Snap lower portion of lens into slots at lens housing.
