Center Mounted Brake Lamp Bulb: Service and Repair

REMOVAL
1. Remove CHMSL from liftgate.
2. Turn bulb socket 1/4 turn counter clockwise.
3. Separate socket from lamp.
4. Grasp bulb and pull from socket.

INSTALLATION
1. Position bulb in socket and press into place.
2. Position socket in lamp.
3. Turn bulb socket 1/4 turn clockwise.
4. Install CHMSL.
