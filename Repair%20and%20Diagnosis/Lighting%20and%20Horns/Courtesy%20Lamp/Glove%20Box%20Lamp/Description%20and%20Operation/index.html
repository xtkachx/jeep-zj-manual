Glove Box Lamp: Description and Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
Circuit A7 from 15 in the Power Distribution Center (PDC) powers circuit M1 through fuse 16 in the junction block. Circuit M1 powers the glove box lamp. The lamp has a switch in series which when closed, connects the lamp to ground on circuit Z1.
