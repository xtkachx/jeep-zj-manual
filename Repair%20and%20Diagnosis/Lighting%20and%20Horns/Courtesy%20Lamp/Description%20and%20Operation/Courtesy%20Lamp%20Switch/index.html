Courtesy Lamp Switch

CIRCUIT OPERATION
When the courtesy lamp switch inside the headlamp switch closes, it completes a path to ground for circuit M11 from the Body Control Module (BCM). The BCM energizes the courtesy lamp relay in the junction block to power the courtesy lamps.
