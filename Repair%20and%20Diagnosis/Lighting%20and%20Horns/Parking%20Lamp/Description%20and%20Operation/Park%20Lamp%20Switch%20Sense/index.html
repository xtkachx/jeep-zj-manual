Park Lamp Switch Sense

CIRCUIT OPERATION
When the operator puts the headlamp switch in the park lamp position, the park lamp switch closes and circuit L90 powers the parking lamps. Circuit L90 also provides an input to the Body Control Module (BCM). The BCM monitors the L90 circuit and circuit 707 from the dimmer switch to determine instrument panel lamp intensity.
