Auto Headlamp Sensor

The auto headlamp sensor is the key sensor for the auto headlamp system. The sensor needs real sun-light to properly register the light level. When auto headlamps are enabled indoors, the headlamps may be turned on. The sensor is located in the center of the defroster grille at the base of the windshield.
