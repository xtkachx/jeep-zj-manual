Auto Headlamps (Ultralight)

AUTO HEADLAMPS (ULTRALIGHT)
The auto headlamps option will operate the same as the 95 ZJ while offering additional features such as Auto Funeral Mode. If enabled, the lights will automatically turn on at dusk after the engine has successfully started. Lights will automatically extinguish if ambient light indicates daytime.
