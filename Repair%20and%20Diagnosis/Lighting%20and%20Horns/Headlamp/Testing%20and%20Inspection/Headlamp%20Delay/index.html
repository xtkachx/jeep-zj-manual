Headlamp Delay

HEADLAMP DELAY
The headlamp delay is active whenever the headlamps are ON and subsequently the ignition is switched off. If the vehicle is equipped with Auto Headlamps, a delay of 90 seconds begins immediately after key-off. If the vehicle does not have Auto Headlamps, the delay begins when the headlamp switch is switched to the OFF position. If 30 seconds elapses before the headlamp switch is switched to the OFF position with ignition OFF, headlamp delay is cancelled for that cycle.
