Circuit Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
Circuits L65 and L64 from the turn signal/hazard flasher circuitry in the multi-function switch power the turn signal indicator lamps. Circuit L64 powers the right turn signal indicator lamp. Circuit L65 powers the left indicator lamp. Circuits Z1 and Z2 provide ground for the lamps.
