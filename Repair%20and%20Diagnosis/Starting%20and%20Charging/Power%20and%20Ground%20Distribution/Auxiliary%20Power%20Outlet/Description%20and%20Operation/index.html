Auxiliary Power Outlet: Description and Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
Circuit A7 from 15 in the Power Distribution Center (PDC) powers circuit F38 through fuse 21 in the junction block. Circuit F38 feeds the power outlet. Circuits A7 and F38 are HOT at all times. Circuit Z1 provides ground for the power outlet.
