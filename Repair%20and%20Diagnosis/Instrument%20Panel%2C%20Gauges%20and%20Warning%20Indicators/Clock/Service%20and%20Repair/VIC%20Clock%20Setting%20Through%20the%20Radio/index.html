VIC Clock Setting Through the Radio

RBR - AM/FM CD, 3 band E.Q., external amplifier:




RBR - AM/FM CD, 3 band E.Q., external amplifier, CCD diagnostics.

RBN - AM/FM cassette, 5 band E.Q., external amplifier:




RBN - AM/FM cassette, 5 band E.Q., external amplifier, equipped to handle external CD changer, CCD diagnostics.

RAZ - AM/FM cassette and CD player, 3 band E.Q., external amplifier:




RAZ - AM/FM cassette and CD player, 3 band E.Q., external amplifier, CCD diagnostics.

Vehicles with radios that communicate with the CCD Buss will display the time on the VIC when the time on the radio is set. To set the time on the radio, press and hold the H (hour) and M (minute) buttons with a small pin until the desired time is reached.

For VIC initial set-up see Driver-Vehicle Information Display/Service and Repair/Service Procedures. Service Procedures
