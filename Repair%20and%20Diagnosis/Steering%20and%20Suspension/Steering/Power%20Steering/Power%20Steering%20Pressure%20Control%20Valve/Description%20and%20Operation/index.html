Power Steering Pressure Control Valve: Description and Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
The speed proportional steering control module (SPSCM) operates the speed proportional steering solenoid. The SPSCM supplies a pulse width modulated voltage to the solenoid. Circuits S99 and S98 connect the SPSCM to the solenoid.
