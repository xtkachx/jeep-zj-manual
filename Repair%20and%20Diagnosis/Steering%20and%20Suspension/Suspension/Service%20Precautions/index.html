Suspension: Service Precautions

NOTE: Periodic lubrication of the front suspension and steering system components is required.

CAUTION: Suspension components with rubber bushings (except stabilizer bar) should be tightened with the vehicle at normal ride height. It is important to have the springs supporting the weight of the vehicle when the fasteners are torqued. If springs are not at their normal ride position, vehicle ride comfort could be affected and premature bushing wear may occur. Rubber bushings must never be lubricated.
