Alignment: Testing and Inspection

PRE-ALIGNMENT INSPECTION
Before starting wheel alignment, the following inspection is necessary and must be completed.
1. Inspect tires for size, air pressure and tread wear.
2. Inspect front wheel bearings for wear or adjustment.
3. Inspect front wheels and tires for excessive radial or lateral runout and balance.
4. Inspect ball studs, linkage pivot points and steering gear for looseness, roughness or binding.
5. Inspect suspension components for wear and noise.
