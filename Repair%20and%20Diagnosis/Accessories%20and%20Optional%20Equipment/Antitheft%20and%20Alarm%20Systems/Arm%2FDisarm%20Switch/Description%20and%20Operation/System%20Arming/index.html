System Arming

CIRCUIT OPERATION
The system alarm sets after the operator uses the power door locks or Remote Keyless Entry (RKE) transmitter to lock the doors and liftgate. After all doors and the liftgate are locked and closed, the BCM illuminates a red Light Emitting Diode (LED) (VTSS indicator light) on circuit G69. The red LED is located on the top of the instrument panel. The LED flashes rapidly signalling the system is arming. It flashes at slower rate after approximately 15 seconds, indicating the BCM has set the VTSS.
