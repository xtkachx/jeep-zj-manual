Keyless Entry Receiver: Service and Repair

To program the Remote Keyless Entry (RKE) transmitter access codes into the RKE receiver in the passenger door module requires the use of a DRB scan tool. Refer to the proper Computers and Control Systems/Body Control Module/Testing and Inspection Procedures for more information.
