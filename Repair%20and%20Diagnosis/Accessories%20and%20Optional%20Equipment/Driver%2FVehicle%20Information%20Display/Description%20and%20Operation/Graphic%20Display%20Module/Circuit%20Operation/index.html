Circuit Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION

Several fuses supply power to the Graphic Display Module (GDM). When the ignition switch is in the RUN position it connects circuit A1 from fuse 8 in the PDC to circuit A22. Circuit A22 feeds circuit F83 through fuse 6 in the junction block. Circuit F83 supplies voltage to the GDM.

Circuit A7 from fuse 15 in the PDC powers circuit F60 through fuse 20 in the junction block. Circuits A7 and F60 are HOT at all times. Circuit F60 feeds the GDM. Circuits Z1 and Z2 provide ground for the GDM.
