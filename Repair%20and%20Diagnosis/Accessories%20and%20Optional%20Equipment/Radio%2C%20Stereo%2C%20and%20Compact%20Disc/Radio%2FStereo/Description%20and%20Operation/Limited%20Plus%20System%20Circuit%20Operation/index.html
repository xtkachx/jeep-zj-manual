Limited Plus System Circuit Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
The circuits for the Limited Plus system are the same as the Premium System except for the sound bar, mounted on the inside roof headliner just forward of the liftgate.

For the speakers housed in the sound bar, circuit X95 from the amplifier is the feed for the left speakers. Circuit X97 is the return to the amplifier for the left speakers. Circuit X98 from the amplifier is the feed for the right speakers. Circuit X96 is the return to the amplifier for the right speakers.
