Amplifier: Service and Repair

REMOVAL
1. Disconnect and isolate the battery negative cable.
2. Disengage the left rear seat cushion latch by pulling upward on the release strap. Tilt the seat cushion forward.
3. Lift the carpeting in the under-seat area as required to access the amplifier.

Amplifier Remove/Install - Typical:




4. Unplug the two wire harness connectors from the amplifier.
5. Remove the three screws that secure the amplifier to the floor.
6. Remove the amplifier.
7. Reverse the removal procedures to install. Tighten the amplifier mounting screws to 2.8 N.m (25 in. lbs.).
