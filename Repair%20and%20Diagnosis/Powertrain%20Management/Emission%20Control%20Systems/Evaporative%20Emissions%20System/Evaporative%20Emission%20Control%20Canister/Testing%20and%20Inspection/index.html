Evaporative Emission Control Canister: Testing and Inspection

If Charcoal Canister is saturated with liquid fuel, check for proper Duty Cycle Purge Solenoid and Pressure/Relief Rollover Valve operation.

NOTE: If charcoal canister is saturated with liquid fuel or cannister is defective in any other way the complete assembly must be replaced.
