Pressure, Vacuum and Temperature Specifications

Fuel System Pressure 339 kPa +/- 34 kPa (49.2 psi +/- 5 psi)

Fuel System Pressure Drop
(fuel pump not engaged) Up to 138 kPa (20 psi)

NOTE: Fuel pressure should stay above 30 psi for 5 minutes.
