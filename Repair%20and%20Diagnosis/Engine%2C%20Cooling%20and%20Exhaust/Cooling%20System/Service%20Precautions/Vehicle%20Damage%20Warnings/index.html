Vehicle Damage Warnings

Engine Accessory Drive Belts

CAUTION: When installing a serpentine accessory drive belt, the belt MUST be routed correctly. If not, the engine may overheat due to water pump rotating in wrong direction. Refer to the appropriate engine Belt Schematic for the correct belt routing. Or, refer to the Belt Routing Label located in the engine compartment.
