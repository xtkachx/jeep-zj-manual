General Engine Specifications
Engine Type In-line 6 Cylinder
Bore 98.4 mm (3.88 in.)
Stroke 86.69 mm (3.413 in.)
Displacement 4.0 liters (242 cu. in.)
Compression Ratio 8.8 : 1
Compression Pressure 827-1034 kPa (120-150 psi)
Maximum Variation Between Cylinders 206 kPa (30 psi)
Torque 305 Nm (225 ft. lbs.) @ 4000 rpm
Firing Order 1-5-3-6-2-4
Lubrication Pressure Feed-Full Flow Filtration
Engine Oil Capacity 5.7L (6 qts)
Cooling System Liquid Cooled-Forced Circulation
Cooling System Capacity 11.4L (12 qts)
Includes 2.2L (2.3 qt) for coolant recovery bottle.
Cylinder Block Cast Iron
Crankshaft Cast Nodular Iron
Cylinder Head Cast Iron
Camshaft Cast Iron
Pistons Aluminum Alloy (with struts)
Combustion Chamber Double Quench
Connecting Rods Cast Iron
