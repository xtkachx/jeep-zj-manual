General Information

MALFUNCTIONS
Engine diagnosis is helpful in determining the causes of malfunctions not detected and remedied by routine tune-ups. These malfunctions may be classified as either mechanical (e.g., a strange noise), or performance (e.g., engine idles rough and stalls). Refer to Performance Charts, for possible causes and corrections of malfunctions. Symptom Related Diagnostic Procedures

ADDITIONAL TESTS
