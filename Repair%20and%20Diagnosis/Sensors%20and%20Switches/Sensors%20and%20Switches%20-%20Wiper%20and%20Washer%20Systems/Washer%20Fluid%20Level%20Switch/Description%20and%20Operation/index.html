Washer Fluid Level Switch: Description and Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
When the switch in the low washer fluid sensor closes, it connects circuit G29 from the Vehicle Information Center (VIC) to ground on circuit Z2. The VIC displays the low washer fluid message.
