Red, Brown, Yellow or White Deposits - Fuel Additives

SCAVENGER DEPOSITS

Scavenger Deposits:




Fuel scavenger deposits may be either white or yellow. They may appear to be harmful, but are a normal condition caused by chemical additives in certain fuels. These additives are designed to change the chemical nature of deposits and decrease spark plug misfire tendencies. Notice that accumulation on the ground electrode and shell area may be heavy but the deposits are easily removed using standard procedures. Spark plugs with scavenger deposits can be considered normal in condition, cleaned and reused.
