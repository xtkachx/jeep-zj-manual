Refrigerant Recycling Device


The Chrysler Corporation recommends that a refrigerant recycling device for R-134a systems, which meets SAE standard J221O, be used. Contact an automotive service equipment supplier for refrigerant recycling equipment that is available in your area. Refer to the operating instructions provided with the recycling equipment for proper operation.
