ABS Warning Lamp Circuit Operation

CIRCUIT OPERATION
Circuit F87 from fuse 5 in the junction block provides power for the ABS warning lamp in the instrument cluster. Ground for the ABS warning lamp is provided by the Controller, Anti-Lock Brakes (CAB). The CAB illuminates the lamp by providing ground on circuit 205.
