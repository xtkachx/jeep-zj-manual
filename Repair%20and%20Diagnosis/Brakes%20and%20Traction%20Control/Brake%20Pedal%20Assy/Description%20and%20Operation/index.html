Brake Pedal Assy: Description and Operation

A suspended-type brake pedal is used, the pedal pivots on a shaft mounted in the pedal support bracket. The bracket is attached to the dash panel. The brake pedal is a serviceable component. The pedal, pedal bushings and shaft are all replaceable parts. The pedal bracket can also be replaced when necessary.
