Door Locks - Cycle When Rear Defogger is Turned Off

NO: 08-34-97

GROUP: Electrical

DATE: Sep. 19, 1997

SUBJECT:
Door Locks Cycle When Rear Window Defogger Is Turned Off

MODELS:
1997 (ZJ) Grand Cherokee

SYMPTOM/CONDITION:

One or more of the power door locks may cycle when the rear window defogger is turned off. The vehicle must be accelerating at speed over 15 MPH when the rear window defogger is turned off.

DIAGNOSIS:

Road test the vehicle. Turn the rear window defogger on. With the vehicle operating at speeds over 15 MPH, depress the accelerator and turn the rear window defogger off while accelerating. If the power door lock cycles, note which door lock activates. If the drivers door activates, replace the drivers door switch module. If the passenger door activates, replace the passenger door switch module.

PARTS REQUIRED:

1 56042462AC Switch Module, Power Window & Door Lock - Right
1 56042463AC Switch Module, Power Window & Door Lock - Left

REPAIR PROCEDURE:

This bulletin involves replacing the power window and door lock switch module.

1. Disconnect and isolate the battery negative cable.

2. Remove the right front door trim panel.

3. Remove the switch module from the door trim panel.

4. Install the new switch module, into the door trim panel.

5. Install the door trim panel.

6. Connect the battery negative cable.

7. If the right hand door switch module was replaced, program the remote keyless entry transmitters.

POLICY: Reimbursable within the provisions of the warranty.

TIME ALLOWANCE:
Labor Operation No: 08-80-77-90 0.3 Hrs.

FAILURE CODE: P8 - New Part
