Compressor Clutch Relay: Description and Operation

COMPRESSOR CLUTCH RELAY

The compressor clutch relay is a International Standards Organization (ISO) micro-relay. The terminal designations and functions are the same as a conventional ISO relay However, the micro-relay terminal orientation (footprint) is different, the current capacity is lower, and the relay case dimensions are smaller than on the conventional ISO relay.

The compressor clutch relay is a electromechanical device that switches current to the compressor clutch coil when the Powertrain Control Module (PCM) grounds the coil side of the relay The PCM responds to inputs from the A/C compressor switch on the heater-A/C control panel, the ATC controller, the low pressure cycling clutch switch, and the high pressure cut-off switch.
