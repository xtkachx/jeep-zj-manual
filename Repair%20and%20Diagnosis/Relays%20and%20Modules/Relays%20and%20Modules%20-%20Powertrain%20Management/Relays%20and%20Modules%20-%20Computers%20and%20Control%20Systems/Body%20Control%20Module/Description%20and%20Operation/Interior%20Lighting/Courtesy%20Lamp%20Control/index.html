Courtesy Lamp Control

COURTESY LAMP CONTROL
The body controller has direct control over the majority of the vehicle's courtesy lamps. The Key Halo lamp is part of the courtesy lamp system and is illuminated whenever the other courtesy lamps are illuminated. The body computer will illuminate the courtesy lamps under any of the following conditions:
1. Any door is ajar.
2. The liftgate or flipper glass is ajar and the courtesy lamp disable is inactive.
3. The courtesy lamp switch on the instrument panel is closed.
4. A Remote Keyless entry unlock message is received.
