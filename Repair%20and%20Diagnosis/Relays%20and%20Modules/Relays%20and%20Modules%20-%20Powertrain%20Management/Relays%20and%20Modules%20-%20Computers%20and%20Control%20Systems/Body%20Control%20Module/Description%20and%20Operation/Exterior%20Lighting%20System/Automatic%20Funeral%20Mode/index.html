Automatic Funeral Mode

AUTOMATIC FUNERAL MODE
If the vehicle has the auto headlamp option, the customer will have the automatic funeral mode feature. If the park lamps or head lamps are manually activated during the daytime (as in a funeral procession), the vacuum fluorescent displays will remain at full daytime brightness to allow for greater visibility. The interior instrumentation lighting will be illuminated at the level of the I/P dimming switch.
