Sun Roof - Inoperative/Stuck: Overview

NO: 23-54-98

GROUP: Body

DATE: Nov. 6, 1998

SUBJECT:
Inoperative Sun Roof

MODELS:

1997 - 1998 (ZJ) Grand Cherokee

SYMPTOM/CONDITION:

The sun roof may be inoperative or stuck partially opened. This may be due to a cable guide tube which has separated from the guide tube bracket. A repair kit has been released to allow for replacement of the tubes instead of assembly replacement.

DIAGNOSIS:

If the sunroof is inoperative or stuck in one position, inspect the cable guide tube. If the tube has separated from the bracket, perform the Repair Procedure.

PARTS REQUIRED:

1 05014766AA Cable tube repair kit.

POLICY: Reimbursable within the provisions of the warranty.

TIME ALLOWANCE:

Labor Operation No:

23-30-64-91 Sunroof Drive Cables/Tubes Replace 1.9 Hrs.
Optional Equipment:

23-30-64-60 Sound Bar Equipped 0.2 Hrs.

FAILURE CODE: P8 - New Part
