Dealer Service Instructions

IMPORTANT

DEALER SERVICE INSTRUCTIONS

Customer Notification # 712 -- Passenger Air Bag Safety

^ This service requirement applies only to following Dodge, Chrysler, Plymouth, Jeep and Eagle vehicles built through February 24, 1997 (MDH 0224XX) equipped with a passenger air bag:

^ 1993-1997 Intrepid, Concorde, New Yorker, LHS and Vision (LH)
^ 1994-1997 Caravan, Voyager and Town & Country (AS/NS)
^ 1994-1995 LeBaron Convertible (AJ)
^ 1994-1996 Stealth (B7)
^ 1995-1996 Summit (B9) and Summit Wagon (B8)
^ 1995-1997 Avenger, Sebring and Talon (FJ)
^ 1995-1997 Neon (PL)
^ 1995-1997 Stratus, Breeze and Cirrus (JA)
^ 1996-1997 Sebring Convertible (JX)
^ 1996-1997 Viper Coupe (SR)
^ 1996-1997 Grand Cherokee (ZJ)
^ 1997 Cherokee (XJ)
^ 1997 Wrangler (TJ)
^ 1997 Dakota (AN)

^ The enclosed owner notification letters advise owners of the above vehicles of the steps that can be taken to minimize the risk of harm from a deploying air bag. Dealers are requested to install sun visor labels on the involved vehicles still in inventory. Note that 1997 model year vehicles equipped with a passenger side air bag built on or after February 25, 1997 (MDH 0225XX) have already had labels installed at the assembly plants.

Parts Packages:

^ Each involved dealer, to whom 1997 model year vehicles in this notification were invoiced, will receive 100 Air Bag Warning Labels to service 50 vehicles.
