Front Brakes - One Click on Initial Application

NO: 05-02-98

GROUP: Brakes

DATE: Mar. 13, 1998

SUBJECT:
Front Brake Click

MODELS:
1997 - 1998 (TJ) Wrangler
1997 - 1998 (XJ) Cherokee
1997 - 1998 (ZJ) Grand Cherokee

SYMPTOM/CONDITION:

A single click can be heard when the driver applies the brakes while traveling forward. The noise will normally occur the first time the brakes are applied while moving forward, following a brake application in reverse. An improved brake pad has been developed with increased tension on the anti-rattle clip to reduce the free play between the pad metal backing and the knuckle.

DIAGNOSIS:

Start the vehicle and apply the brakes while moving slowly in reverse. Put the transmission in drive and apply the brakes after the vehicle starts moving. If the noise is present, perform the Repair Procedure.

PARTS REQUIRED:

1 04883344AB Brake Pad Set
1 J8993704 Brake Lubricant

REPAIR PROCEDURE:

This bulletin involves installing brake pads and lubricating the knuckle.

1. Using the procedures outlined in the appropriate service manual, remove the front calipers and brake pads.

2. Clean the steering knuckle mounting ledge slides with a wire brush. Apply a light coat of brake lubricant, p/n J8993704, to the ledge.

3. Install the new brake pads, pin 04883344AB, and install the calipers.

POLICY: Reimbursable within the provisions of the warranty.

TIME ALLOWANCE:

Labor Operation No: 05-77-22-96 0.6 Hrs.

FAILURE CODE: P8 - New Part
