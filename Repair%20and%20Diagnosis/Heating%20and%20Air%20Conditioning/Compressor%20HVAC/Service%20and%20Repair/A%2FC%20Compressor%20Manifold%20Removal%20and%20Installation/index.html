A/C Compressor Manifold Removal and Installation

REMOVAL
1. Disconnect the negative cable from the battery.
2. Discharge the A/C system into a recovery/recycle device.
Refrigerant
3. Remove the A/C lines from the manifold.
4. Remove the compressor manifold bolts.
5. Remove the manifold.

INSPECTION
Check the manifold seal for damage. Replace seal if damaged.
