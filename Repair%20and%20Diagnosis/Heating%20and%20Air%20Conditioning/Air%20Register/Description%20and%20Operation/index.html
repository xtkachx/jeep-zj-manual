Air Register: Description and Operation


SIDE WINDOW DEMISTERS
The side window demisters direct air from the Heater-A/C assembly. The outlets are located on the top left and right edges of the door panels. The Demisters operate when the control mode selector is on FLOOR, BI-LEVEL, FLOOR/DEFROST or DEFROST mode.
