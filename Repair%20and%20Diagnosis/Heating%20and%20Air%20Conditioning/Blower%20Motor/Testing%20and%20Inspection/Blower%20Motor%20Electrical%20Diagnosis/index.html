Blower Motor Electrical Diagnosis




NOISE

To verify that the blower is the source of the noise, disconnect the blower motor connector and operate the heater-A/C system. If the noise goes away, possible causes include:

- Foreign material in the heater-A/C housing Improper blower motor mounting

- Improper blower wheel mounting Blower motor faulty.
