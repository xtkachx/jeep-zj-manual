Installation

1. Press the blower motor wheel onto the blower motor shaft. Be sure the flat on the blower motor shaft lines up with the flat inside the wheel.
2. Install the retainer clip. The ears of the retainer clip must be over the flat surface on the motor shaft.

Fig.6 Blower Motor Seal:





3. Be sure the seal is installed on the blower motor housing.
4. Install the blower motor and wheel assembly.
5. Install and tighten blower motor and wheel assembly screws.
6. Connect the electrical connector and install into the retainer.
7. Connect the blower motor cooling tube.
8. Connect the negative cable to the battery.
