Removal

Fig.60 Evaporator Core Location In Heater-A/C Housing (Upside Down):





REMOVAL
1. Remove the heater-A/C unit.
Refer to: Housing Assembly HVAC : Service and Repair Service and Repair
2. Turn the heater-A/C unit upside down.
3. Remove the screws that secure the two housing halves together. Unsnap the center adaptor heat duct from the lower housing and remove the screw hidden by the adaptor.
4. Carefully turn the heater-A/C unit over. Remove the top half of the housing.
5. Lift the evaporator core from the heater-A/C unit housing.
