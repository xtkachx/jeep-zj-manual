Accumulator Replacement

ACCUMULATOR
REMOVAL
1. Disconnect and isolate the battery negative cable.
2. Recover the refrigerant.
Refrigerant Recovery

3. Disconnect the refrigerant lines from the accumulator. Install plugs in, or tape over all of the open refrigerant fittings.
4. Unplug the wire harness connector from the low pressure cycling clutch switch.
5. Loosen the screw that secures the accumulator band to the support bracket.
6. Remove the accumulator.

INSTALLATION
1. Install the accumulator band screw in the support bracket.
2. Tighten the band screw in the support bracket.
3. Plug the wire harness connector into the low pressure cycling clutch switch.
4. Remove the plugs or tape from the refrigerant line fittings. Connect the refrigerant lines to the accumulator.
5. Evacuate and charge the refrigerant system.
Refrigerant System Evacuate

6. Connect the battery negative cable.

NOTE: If the accumulator is replaced, add 120 ml (4 oz.) of refrigerant oil to the refrigerant system.
