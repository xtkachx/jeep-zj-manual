Recalibrating ATC Head


Anytime the ATC head or any of the actuators are replaced, the system must be re-calibrated. This is accomplished by disconnecting and reconnecting the battery or by using the DRB and selecting:

-Body Computer
-ATC
-Miscellaneous
-Reset ATC Head.

If the head is changed, simply disconnecting and reconnecting the connector on the back of the head will cause the system to reset, or calibrate, upon power-up.
