Recall - Passenger Air Bag Safety: Overview

No.: 712

April, 1997

To: All Dodge, Chrysler-Plymouth and Jeep(R) & Eagle Dealers

Subject:
Customer Notification # 712 -- Passenger Air Bag Safety

Models:
The Following Dodge, Chrysler, Plymouth, Jeep and Eagle Vehicles Built Through February 24, 1997 (MDH 0224XX) Equipped With a Passenger Air Bag:

^ 1993-1997 Intrepid, Concorde, New Yorker, LHS and Vision (LH)
^ 1994-1997 Caravan, Voyager and Town & Country (AS/NS)
^ 1994-1995 LeBaron Convertible (AJ)
^ 1994-1996 Stealth (B7)
^ 1995-1996 Summit (B9) and Summit Wagon (B8)
^ 1995-1997 Avenger, Sebring and Talon (FJ)
^ 1995-1997 Neon (PL)
^ 1995-1997 Stratus, Breeze and Cirrus (JA)
^ 1996-1997 Sebring Convertible (JX)
^ 1996-1997 Viper Coupe (SR)
^ 1996-1997 Grand Cherokee (ZJ)
^ 1997 Cherokee (XJ)
^ 1997 Wrangler (TJ)
^ 1997 Dakota (AN)

The enclosed owner notification letters advise owners of the above vehicles of the steps that can be taken to minimize the risk of harm from a deploying air bag. One letter was mailed to 1993 through 1996 vehicle owners and one to 1997 vehicle owners. Four (4) air bag warning labels were enclosed with each letter. Owners were requested to install two (2) labels on each sun visor.

Involved dealers should use the enclosed 1997 model year VIN list to install labels on any 1997 models that are still in new vehicle inventory. Note that 1997 model year vehicles equipped with a passenger side air bag built on or after February 25, 1997 (MDH 0225XX) have already had labels installed at the assembly plants.

In addition, dealers should install these labels on the above listed 1993 through 1997 models in used vehicle inventory. Use the above model descriptions to determine which 1993 through 1996 model year vehicles require labels as there will be no VIN lists provided for these vehicles.

Details of this service action are explained in the following sections.

Service Procedure Videotape

No videotape of the service procedure for this notification will be provided.

Dealer Notification & Vehicle List

Involved dealers:
Each dealer to whom involved 1997 model year vehicles were invoiced (or the current dealer at the same street address) will receive a copy of this dealer notification letter and a list of the involved 1997 model year vehicles by first class mail.

The 1997 model year Vehicle List is arranged in Vehicle Identification Number (VIN) sequence. Owners known to Chrysler are also listed. The lists are for dealer reference in arranging for service of involved vehicles still in dealer inventory.

All other dealers:
Each Dodge, Chrysler-Plymouth and Jeep & Eagle dealer who does not receive a Vehicle List will receive a copy of this dealer notification letter by first class mail.

DIAL System Functions 53, 70 and VIP

Vehicles will NOT be entered to DIAL System Functions 53, 70 or VIP.

Parts

Each involved dealer, to whom 1997 model year vehicles in the notification were invoiced (or the current dealer at the same street address), will receive and be billed for, 100 Air Bag Warning Labels, PN 4897875AA. Each label part number is a sheet that contains one large label and one small label. Additional labels may be ordered as needed to support customer demand. There is a minimum sales quantity of six (6) labels per order, or enough labels for three vehicles.

Owner Notification and Service Scheduling

All involved vehicle owners known to Chrysler have been notified of the service requirement by first class mail. They were requested to install the Air Bag Warning labels themselves. Copies of the owner notification letters are included.
