Recall 97V080000: Unexpected Air Bag Deployment

The electrical circuit design allows the potential for an inadvertent air bag deployment upon vehicle ignition shut down.

Unexpected air bag deployment can result in occupant injury.

Dealers will replace the air bag electronic control module with a newly designed module. Owner notification began during July 1997. Owners who take their vehicles to an authorized dealer on an agreed upon service date and do not receive the free remedy within a reasonable time should contact Chrysler at 1-800-853-1403. Also contact the National Highway Traffic Safety Administration's Auto Safety Hotline
at 1-800-424-9393.
