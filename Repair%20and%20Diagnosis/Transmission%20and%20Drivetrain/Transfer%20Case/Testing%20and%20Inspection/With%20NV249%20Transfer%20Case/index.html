With NV249 Transfer Case

CONDITION
TRANSFER CASE DIFFICULT TO SHIFT OR WILL NOT SHIFT INTO DESIRED RANGE

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Vehicle speed too great to permit shifting.
2. If vehicle was operated for extended period in 4H mode on dry paved surface, driveline torque load may cause difficulty.
3. Transfer case external shift linkage binding.
4. Insufficient or incorrect lubricant.
5. Internal components binding, worn or damaged.

CORRECTION
1. Stop vehicle and shift into desired range. Or reduce speed to 3-A km/h (2-3 mph) before attempting to shift.
2. Stop vehicle, shift transmission to neutral, shift transfer case to 2H mode and operate vehicle on 2H on dry paved surface.
3. Lubricate, repair or replace linkage, or tighten loosen components as necessary.
4. Drain and refill to edge of fill hole with MOPAR ATF PLUS (Type 9602 ) or DEXRON II Automatic Transmission Fluid.
5. Disassemble unit and replace worn or damaged components as necessary.



CONDITION
TRANSFER CASE NOISY IN ALL DRIVE MODES

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Insufficient or incorrect lubricant.

CORRECTION
1. Drain and refill to edge of fill hole with MOPAR ATF PLUS (Type 9602 ) or DEXRON II Automatic Transmission Fluid. Check for leaks and repair if necessary. If unit is still noisy after drain and refill, disassembly and inspection may be required to locate source of noise.



CONDITION
NOISY IN-OR JUMPS OUT OF FOUR-WHEEL-DRIVE LOW RANGE

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Transfer case not completely engaged in AL position.
2. Shift linkage loose or binding.
3. Range fork cracked, inserts worn, or fork is binding on shift rail.
4. Annulus gear or lockplate worn or damaged

CORRECTION
1. Stop vehicle, shift transfer case to Neutral, then shift back into AL position.
2. Tighten, lubricate, or repair linkage as necessary.
3. Disassemble unit and repair as necessary
4. Disassemble unit and repair as necessary



CONDITION
LUBRICANT LEAKING FROM OUTPUT SHAFT SEALS OR FROM VENT

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Transfer case overfilled.
2. Vent closed or restricted.
3. Output shaft seals damaged or installed correctly.

CORRECTION
1. Drain to correct level.
2. Clear or replace vent if necessary.
3. Replace seals. Be sure seal lip faces interior of case when installed. Also be sure yoke seal surfaces are not scored or nicked. Remove scores and nicks with fine sandpaper or replace yoke(s) if necessary.



CONDITION
ABNORMAL TIRE WEAR

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Extended operation on dry hard surface (paved) roads in 4H range.

CORRECTION
1. Operate in 2H on hard surface (paved) roads.
