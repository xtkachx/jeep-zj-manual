Shift Forks and Mainshaft Removal





1. Remove detent plug, O-ring, detent spring and detent plunger.
2. Remove mainshaft from clutch sleeve and input gear pilot bearing.





3. Rotate shift sector so sector teeth face upward.





4. Remove range fork, rail and clutch sleeve as assembly. Lift shift rail upward, rotate fork out of shift sector and remove assembly.





5. Remove shift sector. Rotate and tilt sector as needed to remove it.





6. Remove shift sector bushing and O-ring.
