Shift Cable: Testing and Inspection

Gearshift cable adjustment is important because it positions the valve body manual valve. Incorrect adjustment will cause creeping in Neutral, premature clutch wear, delayed engagement in any gear, or a no-start in Park or Neutral position.

Proper operation of the [1][2]park/neutral position switch will provide a quick check of cable adjustment.
