Front

REMOVAL
1. Remove differential case from axle housing.





2. Remove the bearings from the differential case with Puller/Press C-293-PA, C-293-39 Adapter Blocks, and Plug SP-3289, or equivalents.

NOTE: If replacement differential side bearings or differential case are being installed, differential side bearing shim requirements may change. Refer to the Differential Bearing Preload and Gear Backlash procedures to determine the proper shim selection.

INSTALLATION
1. Install differential side bearing shims onto differential case hubs.





2. Using Installer C-3716-A and Handle C-4171, or equivalents, install differential side bearings.
3. Install differential in axle housing.
