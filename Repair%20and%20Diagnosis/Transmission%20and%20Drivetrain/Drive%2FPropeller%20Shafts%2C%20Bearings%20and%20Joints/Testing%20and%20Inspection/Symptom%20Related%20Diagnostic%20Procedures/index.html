Symptom Related Diagnostic Procedures

DRIVE CONDITION
PROPELLER SHAFT

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. Undercoating or other foreign material on shaft.
2. Loose U-joint clamp screws.
3. Loose or bent U-joint yoke or excessive runout.
4. Incorrect drive line angularity.
5. Rear spring center bolt not in seat.
6. Worn U-joint bearings.
7. Propeller shaft damaged (bent tube) or out of balance.
8. Broken rear spring.
9. Excessive runout or unbalanced condition.
10. Excessive drive pinion gear shaft yoke runout.

CORRECTION
1. Clean exterior of shaft and wash with solvent.
2. Tighten screws properly.
3. Install replacement yoke.
4. Correct angularity
5. Loosen spring U~bolts and seat center bolts.
6. Replace U-joint.
7. Install replacement propeller shaft.
8. Replace rear spring.
9. Reindex propeller shaft 180°, test and correct as necessary.
10. Reindex propeller shaft 180° and evaluate.



DRIVE CONDITION
UNIVERSAL JOINT NOISE

POSSIBLE CAUSE
1. U-joint clamp screws loose.
2. Lack of lubrication.

CORRECTION
1. Tighten screws with specified torque.
2. Replace U-joint.
