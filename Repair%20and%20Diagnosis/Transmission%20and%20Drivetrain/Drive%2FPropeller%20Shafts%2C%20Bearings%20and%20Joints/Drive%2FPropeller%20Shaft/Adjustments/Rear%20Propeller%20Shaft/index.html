Rear Propeller Shaft

AXLE PINION ANGLE ADJUSTMENT
A cam kit is available to be installed in the rear axle lower suspension arms in order to provide adjustability of the pinion angle. Follow the procedures supplied with the kit in order to ensure a safe installation.
