Sun Shade: Service and Repair

SUNSHADE

REMOVAL

1. Remove wind deflector, mechanism covers and glass panel.
2. Position system to full rearward position.
3. Slide sunshade panel full forward and release the front tabs from track assembly.






4. Pull front and rear retaining clips inboard and lift sunshade out.

INSTALLATION

1. Reverse removal procedure.
